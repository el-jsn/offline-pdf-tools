## Fix for PDFs skipped during import

PDFs are no longer rejected solely because QPDF finished reading their metadata with warnings. The importer accepts warning-only inspection results when the returned metadata is valid.

Fatal inspection errors and invalid metadata are still rejected. Repair checks and strict validation of saved outputs remain in place.

Copied error reports also identify normal encryption checks more clearly and recognize additional warning categories.

[Download for Apple Silicon Macs](https://github.com/el-jsn/offline-pdf-tools/releases/download/v1.0.4/PDF-Tools_1.0.4_macos-arm64.dmg), or choose **Check for updates**.
