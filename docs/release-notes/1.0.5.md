## Filled forms work with page tools

Merge, Split, and Organize now prepare saved form answers automatically when you choose **Flatten forms**, instead of blocking forms that need their appearance refreshed.

Multiline text, dropdowns, checkboxes, and radio buttons are prepared locally. Original documents remain unchanged. Forms that cannot be prepared safely still stop with a copyable error report.

[Download for Apple Silicon Macs](https://github.com/el-jsn/offline-pdf-tools/releases/download/v1.0.5/PDF-Tools_1.0.5_macos-arm64.dmg), or choose **Check for updates**.
